# Tesla Silicon and Systems Validation Internship Study Guide

> [!abstract] Role summary
> This internship combines **post-silicon validation**, **board bring-up**, **embedded firmware**, **computer architecture**, **high-speed interface testing**, **power and performance characterization**, **failure analysis**, and **automated test infrastructure**.
>
> The goal is to find silicon, firmware, PCB, power, signal-integrity, and system-integration problems before hardware enters production.
